FIELD NOTES / 01 — THE QUESTION

What if both satellites are right—and the picture is still incomplete?

MODIS and VIIRS can observe different thermal signals because of pixel size, timing and conditions. A hotspot is an observation, not a verified wildfire. Comparing raw counts as if they were the same measure can mislead.
