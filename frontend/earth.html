SCIENCE / NOT AN OPERATIONAL WARNING SYSTEM

Signals, not certainty.

The NASA GIBS vegetation and aerosol overlays are satellite visualizations. Air-quality concentrations and seven-day weather come from external numerical forecasts. A configurable descriptive index compares future weather conditions; it has not been trained or validated to predict fires. Route results don't include official closures or fire-aware evacuation safety.
