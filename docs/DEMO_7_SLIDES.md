# Seven-slide official demo storyboard

These are the seven slides in the editable PowerPoint stored beside this file:

1. **One planet, two satellite stories** — Team Parallax Labs; challenge and value proposition; fill in registered member names.
2. **Meet Maya** — a wildfire-preparedness analyst needs a consistent interpretation of two satellite records.
3. **Real NASA evidence, not simulated fires** — 547,021 Alberta 2023 raw observations; type-gate 542,775 presumed vegetation-fire records, 4,246 excluded other sources.
4. **The signature reveal** — two satellite sides; 1,084 MODIS-only, 6,742 VIIRS-only and 9,268 both active grid-days under the common Equal Earth 10-km UTC grid.
5. **From reveal to season calendar** — show how selecting a month and a custom region updates evidence and brief; not a predicted fire date.
6. **World Atlas** — global interactive interface and optional recent NASA feed. **Only Alberta 2023 is historically embedded**, no global historical coverage claim.
7. **Impact, limits and mission brief** — What Maya can decide, and why provenance and unknowns matter. Sources/AI acknowledgment.
