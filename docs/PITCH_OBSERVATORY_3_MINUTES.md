# Parallax BLINDSPOT — three-minute live story

**Audience:** Maya, a wildfire preparedness analyst planning monitoring and research in Alberta. **Problem:** two NASA satellite systems report different hotspot observations. **Solution:** reveal where their records overlap or diverge; inspect history and uncertainty; export a defensible readiness brief.

## 0:00–0:25 — The house

On `/`, point to the lit observatory: **“Meet Maya. She's preparing for the next wildfire season, but the two satellite archives on her desk disagree. Which pattern can she trust?”** Click **Enter the observatory** or the glowing door.

## 0:25–0:55 — Inside

On `/observatory`, click through the first two scenes: **“MODIS and VIIRS are different instruments. More detections are not necessarily more fires.”** Advance to the gap scene: **“We cannot call every difference a missed fire. We can reveal exactly where evidence diverges.”**

## 0:55–1:55 — The reveal

Click **Open the evidence desk**. On `/explore#reveal`, select **Alberta · 2023 archive loaded**, **May–October 2023**, **Real FIRMS**. Drag the map slider. Switch **Illustrated atlas / Esri satellite** for geographical context. Click **Reveal Blindspots**. Explain common cell-day presence and observed categories, *not* confirmed missed fires. Draw a focus box if time permits.

## 1:55–2:35 — Evidence into a decision

Jump to the **Fire Calendar**. Pick a month to see map selections change, then the **Evidence Desk** where insufficient multi-year records are explicitly labeled. Mention agency context only if a real agency CSV has been imported.

## 2:35–3:00 — Mission brief and one-liner

Show **Fire DNA / Readiness Brief**. **“We did not build another red-dot fire map. We built an investigation tool that shows what two satellites saw, what they disagree on, and what an analyst should verify before planning.”**

**Scientific disclosure:** The bundled archive is Alberta 2023, not a worldwide historical archive; 10-km grid-day alignment is not full orbital/sensor calibration; no ignition prediction is claimed. **Formal NASA submission demo** may require a separate ≤30-second video or ≤7-slide deck under the 2026 project guide.
